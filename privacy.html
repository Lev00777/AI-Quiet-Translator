Privacy Policy

Effective July 19, 2026  ·  Last updated July 19, 2026  ·  Data controller: NeuroOrbit AI

Quiet Translator is built as a zero-retention application. Voice input is processed in real time and displayed on screen — audio is never recorded, saved, or stored on our servers or on your device.

// 01

Microphone access and audio

Quiet Translator needs access to your device microphone solely to provide real-time speech translation during an active session.

Your audio is streamed over encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS) to the speech-recognition providers listed in Section 06, where it is transcribed into text in real time.

We do not save, record, or store any audio files — on your device or on our servers. Audio is streamed in real time and discarded immediately after processing. Our server-side audio retention is 0 days.

Speech-recognition and translation providers may process audio transiently to deliver the service. We do not control their internal processing and encourage you to review their privacy policies. We select providers that do not retain audio for training after processing where such options are available.

// 02

No transcript storage

Translated text is displayed on screen during your active session only. We do not store transcripts in any database, log, or file after the session ends. Server-side and on-device transcript retention: 0 days.

// 03

Data we collect

Account data

  • Email address (via Firebase Authentication)
  • A pseudonymous user identifier
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive basic account identifiers from those providers; we do not receive or store your password

Usage data

  • Listening time used, in minutes
  • Your current subscription plan and remaining minutes
  • Language pairs used (for example, English → Ukrainian)
  • App version and device operating system, for diagnostics

Subscription data

Your subscription status, plan, purchase and renewal events, as provided by Apple, Google, and our subscription-management provider. We do not receive or store your full payment card details — payment is handled by Apple and Google.

We do not collect the content of your conversations.

Retention: account and usage data are kept while your account is active and for up to 24 months after your last activity, then permanently deleted. You may delete your account at any time (Section 07).

Because Quiet Translator does not store audio or transcripts, no recording of other people is created or retained by us.

Your rights and account deletion

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, and CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to access, correct, erase, and port your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

Account deletion. You can delete your account directly in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion removes your account and associated usage data from our systems. Subscription cancellation is managed through your Apple or Google account settings.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete it, and the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell your personal information.

Requests: mission@neuroorbitai.com. We respond within 30 days (45 days for CCPA requests). You may also file a complaint with your local supervisory authority — for example your EU Member State DPA, the UK ICO, the CNIL, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the ANPD.
